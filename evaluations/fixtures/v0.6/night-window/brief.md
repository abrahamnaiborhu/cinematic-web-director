# Synthetic Night Window brief

Night Window offers small-group, astronomer-led sky-observation evenings for adult beginners. This fictional fixture establishes no actual operator, credentials, operating dates or completed bookings. Visitors should understand the evening, judge whether it suits them and find practical information or request a place.

Confirmed format: a short orientation, guided telescope observation and time for questions. Sessions are planned for 90 minutes and groups of up to eight. Visibility depends on conditions; no particular object or guaranteed view is promised. A real operator must supply weather/rescheduling policy, location, dates, price, physical-access information and booking details before publication.

Desired character: curious, expansive and thoughtful, without making people work to find essential information. No visual style, navigation choreography or animation solution is chosen. It is a new landing page, not a portfolio. Phones, compact/short laptop windows and larger screens matter. Useful destinations are /sessions, /visit and /request; these are proposed routes, not tested endpoints. No live booking, motion prototype, implementation, dependency installation or publication is approved.
