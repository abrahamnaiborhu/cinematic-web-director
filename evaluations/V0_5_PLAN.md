# v0.5 Evaluation and Release Plan

This plan executes the client-approved v0.5 priorities: behavioral evaluations, a concrete creative review package, asset/imagery direction, visitor understanding and runtime acceptance. Inputs are synthetic fixtures, not real client biographies or product claims. Nothing in this plan authorizes publishing or editing a client's live website.

## Controlled discovery trials

Use [the design evaluation procedure](DESIGN_DIRECTION.md#running-an-evaluation). The three input projects are [portfolio](fixtures/v0.5/portfolio/brief.md), [landing page](fixtures/v0.5/landing/brief.md) and [product website](fixtures/v0.5/product/brief.md). Give each fresh session the corresponding `request.txt`, raw files and selected skill snapshot. Keep this plan, reviewer criteria, other responses and prior conclusions out of the evaluated context.

Baseline: v0.4.1, commit `1a59610`. Candidate: record the exact revision/snapshot used in the result report. Request the inherited current host configuration without an override for both runs. Record actual model/reasoning identity only when exposed; an inherited request is not independently verified model identity. Treat comparisons as small qualitative observations, not a statistical success rate or model ranking.

Run an initial director response. Supply a further scripted client reply when clarification or progression is needed, drawing only on the fixture facts. Preserve the actual reply in the report. Do not supply motion ideas, the review criteria or an intended answer. Discovery-only sessions may inspect files and write proposals in their isolated output directory; no website/prototype coding, installs, deployments, external messages or nested agents are authorized.

These trials examine bounded discovery behavior. They do not establish end-to-end site delivery, rendered design quality, cross-host portability or real-client satisfaction.

## Reviewer criteria

Inspect actual responses/artifacts for:

- Subject-specific interpretation, factual restraint and required journey/function preservation.
- Useful director-originated concepts/motion proposals before the client invents effects; a concrete recommendation and answerable question.
- Meaningful alternatives, understandable temporal descriptions and appropriately scoped approvals.
- Asset needs, actual versus proposed material, and a practical path to inspectable composition/motion evidence.
- Phone/compact-laptop/large-screen intent and honest browser/device limitations.
- Visitor understanding tasks and observable runtime acceptance, adapted to the assignment rather than a generic checklist.

Record **observed**, **partially observed**, **not observed** or **not applicable**, with evidence and limits. A missing first-turn deliverable may be a legitimate discovery stage; inspect the follow-up before calling it a failure. Do not count headings, infer approvals from file existence or treat text proposals as rendered proof. Reviewer judgment and independent actor execution are separate evidence scopes.

## One bounded motion study

Select a concrete opportunity after examining proposals. Before coding, record the named question, content, start/change/settled states, files/dependencies, host configuration, devices/input alternatives, evidence, iteration boundary and stopping condition. Obtain the required scoped approval. An evaluation fixture can contain synthetic client decisions, but must not be described as a real client choosing or approving a design.

Keep the study isolated from production. Evaluate an actual rendered slice at representative phone, compact-laptop and larger viewports, including reduced-motion and relevant interaction checks. Record emulation, missing real-device/browser access and performance-measurement limits. Visitor-understanding observations require actual responses from a person or an explicitly labeled independent synthetic review.
