A few ways a website can feel.

Borrow qualities. Keep your own story.
01

Editorial & considered

Reading rhythm, a clear hierarchy and room for the subject to breathe.

Notice the type contrast and measured spacing. Would this feel assured, or too reserved?

02

Raw & structural

Exposed structure, assertive typography and very little visual politeness.

Is the appeal the force, the visible grid, or the roughness? Those can be chosen separately.

03

Quiet & object-led

The work has the floor. The interface recedes through restraint and generous space.

Do you like the focus on the object, the stillness, or both? A quiet composition can still move boldly.

04

Expressive & typographic

Words become the image. Scale and rhythm give the page a strong authored voice.

Would this energy suit your message? Which information must stay quiet and easy to read?

05

Playful & eclectic

Unexpected pairings and small irregularities make the page feel sociable and personal.

Does the looseness feel inviting or distracting? You can borrow its warmth without the collage.

06

Tactile & handmade

Illustration, material color and irregular marks bring a human hand into the interface.

Is it the texture, the intimacy or the imperfections you like? This needn’t mean a rustic brand.

07

Precise & systematic

Alignment, labels and visible relationships express order and technical confidence.

Would this help your subject feel understandable, or too clinical? Precision can carry personality too.

08

Experimental & art-led

An image-led composition invites interpretation, with familiar order deliberately interrupted.

Do you want this much ambiguity? Where should visitors still get a completely direct answer?

Separate choices

How far should we push?

You can want precise structure and maximum eccentricity. A bold look can have quiet motion. These preferences help the director ask better questions; they don’t choose a template or approve a build.

“Maximum” invites ambitious ideas and explicit tradeoffs. Readability, access, phone usability and resource limits still need deliberate solutions.

Eccentricity / willingness to surprise
Artistic expression / how much the site is the work
Motion appetite / a separate conversation

This guide does not demonstrate animation, pacing or interaction feel. Those need discussion and, when agreed, a bounded motion study.

Bring this back to the director

Your taste, in your words.

For example: “The clarity of 01, the scale of 04, none of the red. Very eccentric, but let people browse freely.”

Every example is a starting point. Other tastes, your own references, contradictions and “I’m not sure” are all useful.
